Multi-agent signal routing and distributed task coordination. The layer connects specialized agents, routes signals, and manages execution without a single point of failure.

✦Authenticity
HUMAN-LIKE

27 behavioral patterns. 98%+ detector resistance. Intelligence that feels and reads as human in real production environments, not just benchmarks.

State that survives sessions and compounds over time. Agents remember, learn, and build on prior interactions instead of starting from zero each time.

Intelligence that remembers and compounds. Not data storage, but experiential understanding that grows richer with each interaction and decision cycle.
