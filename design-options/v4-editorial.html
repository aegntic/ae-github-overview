The coordination layer for persistent human–AI intelligence.

Once you see the skeleton, everything else becomes obvious. What looked like a portfolio of experiments — a hundred and twenty-seven repositories, a dozen products, a scattering of agents — was always one organism with many organs.

Each organ implements a primitive. Together the primitives form a layer that persists across sessions, processes, and operators, that authenticates outputs adversarially, that holds memory as a substrate, and that keeps sovereignty with the human at the centre.

This is not a SaaS. It is not a research lab. It is the infrastructure for a category that did not exist before agents needed to remember each other.
